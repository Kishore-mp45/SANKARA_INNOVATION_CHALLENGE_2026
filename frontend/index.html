How It Works

Four integrated layers that transform raw hospital data into actionable operational intelligence.

1
Computer Vision Detection

Existing CCTV feeds are processed by AI models to detect and count people across hospital zones in real time.

2
Event-Driven Workflow Updates

Patient stage transitions and department actions trigger automated workflow updates across the system instantly.

3
Real-Time Dashboard Sync

Occupancy data, alerts, and patient status synchronize across all role-based dashboards via live polling.

4
Predictive Intelligence

Machine learning models forecast admission spikes, wait times, and bottlenecks to enable proactive resource allocation.

Core Functionalities

Purpose-built modules designed to address critical operational challenges in hospital environments.

Live Occupancy Monitoring

Track real-time person counts and density levels across every hospital department to prevent overcrowding and optimize patient distribution.

Zone Heatmaps

Visualize congestion patterns through color-coded intensity maps, enabling staff to identify bottlenecks and redirect patient flow proactively.

Predictive Analytics

AI-driven forecasts for peak hours, admission surges, and wait times using trained models on historical operational data.

Resource Allocation

Monitor bed availability, staffing levels, and departmental capacity to make data-driven resource decisions that reduce patient wait times.

Role-Based Access Control

Secure, purpose-built views for Administrators, Doctors, Staff, and Patients — each seeing only what is relevant to their role.

Live Camera Feeds

Low-latency video streams from hospital departments provide direct visual oversight for real-time situational awareness.
